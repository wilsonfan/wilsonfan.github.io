Sous Vide Time & Temperature Reference

Working notes from trial and error, not a finished cookbook — kept as-is because the comparisons are the useful part. Background reading: The Danger Zone Reevaluated.

Poultry — chicken

CutTemp / timeNotes
Whole (spatchcock)68°C / 5–6 hrs—
Whole (in bag)65°C / 3–6 hrsDark and white meat cook at different rates — better to cook thighs and breasts separately.
Whole76°C / 90 min—
Thigh (medium)64.5°C / 3.5 hrsTry 66°C / 4 hrs next.
Thigh (medium)65.0°C / 4 hrsSlightly red in the center.
Thigh (medium)67.0°C / 3 hrsNot much red, but meat a little mushy.
Thigh (medium)69.0°C / 2.5 hrsNext one to try.
Thigh70°C / 60 minAdd oil to the bag.
Thigh73°C / 2–2.5 hrsFor a 0.9 kg bird.
Breast63.0°C / 2 hrs—
Drumstick (cut open)66°C / 3 hrsStill some red, texture still a little mushy.

Thigh bag: one thigh per bag with ½ cup water, 1 tsp salt, spring onion, ginger slices.

Cheng-du style cold chicken (boneless breast): pour boiling water over the brined skin, then dunk in an ice water, pat dry, and sous vide dry (no liquid in the bag).

Hainan chicken rice references: steamykitchen.com, nomss.com, foodcanon.com, itsmydish.com. Also: noshon.it — sous vide chicken thigh.
